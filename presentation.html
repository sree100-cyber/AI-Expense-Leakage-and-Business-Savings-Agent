🧠 AI & Algorithms

Evidence-Based Detection Logic

Clear explanations accompany every flag — no black-box accusations.

👥

Duplicate Payment Logic

Exact Match: Identical non-null payment reference numbers.

Proximity Match: Same vendor + same amount within ±3 days window. High/Medium confidence scoring prevents false accusations.

🌲

Isolation Forest & Z-Score

Statistical: Z-score > 2.5 globally, per-category, and per-vendor.

Machine Learning: Unsupervised Isolation Forest (200 trees, 5% contamination) evaluates multi-feature vectors (amounts + encoded vendors/categories).

📊

Spending Surge Detection

Tracks category monthly momentum; flags sudden spikes ≥25% over the previous period with dollar-amount impact.

🔄

Recurring Subscription Audit

Identifies multi-month vendors; coefficient of variation (<0.15) distinguishes fixed SaaS tiers from variable operational costs.
